Test situations

Each situation opens the sim in a set state from a small file (sim.html?config=tests/…json): a place, a sea, the paddler, the light and the camera. They are what the scorecards are measured on, and what we ask people to look at. Open one, press Space to start, and look for what it says. A situation marked from stage N tests something not built yet: what it shows now is the baseline.
