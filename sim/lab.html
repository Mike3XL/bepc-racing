Sea state editor

A sea state is a stack of layers: wind seas, swells and ripples (with gusts), carried by one current. Each layer has its main controls first; switch any off, change it or add another. The model on the right is what a saved or shared sea contains.
Plan view, 360 m across, north up. Arrows: the direction each layer travels toward.

Use it

Edit the JSON and click out of the box to apply it.
