Surfski downwind simulator

Paddle a surfski through different sea states and learn to read, catch and hold waves. An experiment in whether a simulator can teach, and entertain.

1 · Pick a seaChoose a scenario in the panel, then use the sea-state sliders to make it bigger, smaller or messier.
2 · Pick a paddlerNovice, Advanced or Elite: each has its own boat, power, endurance and stroke.
3 · PaddleSteer onto the face of a wave, power up to catch it, ease off to stay on it. Switch cameras with V.

About this project

A research prototype: the waves, the boat and the paddlers are simplified models tuned to feel plausible, not a certified training device. Works best in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer with a graphics chip; on a phone use the on-screen buttons.
